Bigger task can seem daunting to a junior programmer. But every big task is just a collection of a lot of smaller tasks so the thing to do to help you out is easy: Split up the tasks into small manageable tasks. For some people this happens automatically as they go through the steps in their head but others have to put some more manual effort in. And don't worry, you will get better at this. Think about what your goal is and then list all the steps that are needed to reach said goal. Then follow it step by step, adapt where needed, check off every step you complete (it's a tiny victory!) and before you know it, you're done!
