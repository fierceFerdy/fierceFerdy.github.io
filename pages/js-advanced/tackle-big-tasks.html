Tackle Big Tasks

Bigger task can seem daunting to a junior programmer. But every big task is really a collection of a lot of smaller tasks. Thus, the thing to do to help yourself easy: Split up the tasks into small manageable tasks.

For some people this happens automatically as they go through the steps in their head but others have to put some more manual effort in. In any case, don't worry, you will get better at this.

First, think about what your goal is and then list all the steps that are needed to reach said goal. You don't have to do this in order. Write everything down that pops up in your head, even ideas. Eventually start structuring it into a logical order and keep adding as you get new ideas.

When that's done, you're ready to start! Follow your list step by step, adapt where needed, check off every step you complete (it's a tiny victory, and they feels good!) and before you know it, you're done!

If you get stuck, don't worry about it! Just start on what you can, you'll get there :)

Reflection

You finished, great! Let's look at what we can improve.

Are your steps too big?

Bad examples:
  • “Make the counter”
  • “Handle the button”
  • “Update everything”
Better:
  • “Select the button”
  • “Store the current number”
  • “Increase the value”
  • “Change the text on the page”

Did you make proper use of sub-steps?

Some steps are more complex than others and require multiple steps to complete. It's important to break those down as well to make sure you don't miss anything and to make it easier to follow.

Can another student follow your steps without asking questions?

If not, try to make your steps more clear and detailed. When you feel comfortable sharing, do so!
