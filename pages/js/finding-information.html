This page is heavily in progress and is not a good source of information at the moment

When you need to know something that you don't, you have a few options:

  • You ask another student
  • You ask the teacher
  • You check our documentation/manual
  • You search the web
  • You ask AI how to do the specific thing

With each option, you have to keep in mind:

  • Will they/it teach me properly?
  • Will they give me a short term solution or will they actually teach me something?
  • How do I talk to them?
