When you need to know something that you don't, you have a few options:

  • You ask another student
  • You ask the teacher
  • You check our documentation/manual
  • You search the web
  • You ask AI how to do the specific thing

Each option has its advantages and drawbacks. Thus, you have to keep in mind:

  • Will they/it teach me properly?
  • Will they give me a short term solution or will they actually teach me something?
  • How do I talk to them?

How to ask good questions

To other students and teachers

People can't help you properly if your question is too vague.

Bad question:
  • “My code doesn't work.”
Better question:
  • “My button click works, but the number on the page does not update.”

The second question gives useful information:

  • What already works
  • What doesn't work
  • What feature is involved
